Frequently asked questions

The practical questions, answered.

How do you maintain quality and accountability?+

We start by aligning on role outcomes, working instructions and communication expectations. Your pod includes an on-site shift manager, with progress and service expectations agreed during onboarding.

Can the team work with US, UK and Australian time zones?+

Shift schedules depend on the roles and coverage you need. We scope an appropriate Cebu-based schedule with you before presenting a proposal; exact hours and any shift premiums are confirmed in writing.

How do you approach data security?+

Hardware configuration, dedicated IP/VPN options, confidentiality commitments and access expectations are reviewed for each engagement. Ask for the applicable controls, privacy terms and security documentation before sharing sensitive data.

What does the 30-day replacement policy actually cover?+

If a placed team member is not the right fit within the initial 30 days, we'll work with you on a replacement at no additional placement fee, subject to the service agreement. Request the written policy and eligibility terms before signing.

Is a team operational in 14 days guaranteed?+

Fourteen days is the target deployment timeline for a scoped engagement, subject to role requirements, candidate availability, onboarding inputs and contract terms. Your written proposal confirms the timeline and any applicable payment guarantee.

How does the 30-day risk-free offer work?+

The offer is subject to the final service agreement. Ask us to confirm the exact start date, payment terms, exclusions and remedy in writing before engagement.
